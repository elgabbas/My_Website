<p style="text-align: justify;">The Desert Beauty <i>Calopieris eulimene</i> is reported for the first time from Egypt based on observations from the Gebel Elba region in the southeastern Eastern Desert. The species was recorded in arid desert wadis associated with the presence of the larval host plant <i>Capparis decidua</i>. These findings extend the known distribution of this rare Afrotropical butterfly and highlight the biogeographical significance of the Gebel Elba area as an important refuge for desert biodiversity in Egypt.</p>
